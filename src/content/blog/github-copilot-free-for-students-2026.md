If you write code for your course, GitHub Copilot is still free for verified students. The student offer looks different now, though. In March 2026 GitHub moved students onto a new **Copilot Student** plan, removed manual access to some premium models, and changed how usage is counted from 1 June.

This guide covers who qualifies, how to claim the plan, what you actually get, and what to do if your account still says "Copilot Free".

> **Check this first: sign-up availability.** In mid-2026, several outlets and a [GitHub community thread](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/198236) reported that GitHub had *temporarily paused* new sign-ups for some Copilot plans, including the student plan. Later reports say sign-ups have reopened. Before you start, look at the official [Copilot plans page](https://github.com/features/copilot/plans) and the [Copilot Student docs](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/copilot-on-github/set-up-copilot/enable-copilot/set-up-for-students). If they still mention a pause, you can usually get verified for the Student Developer Pack now and activate Copilot once sign-ups reopen.

## What changed in 2026

### March: the Copilot Student plan

On 13 March 2026, GitHub's [changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-03-13-updates-to-github-copilot-for-students/) announced: "Starting today, students with GitHub Education benefits are now on the new GitHub Copilot Student plan." GitHub also changed which models students can use at the same time.

According to GitHub's [official FAQ](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/111352), the Student plan "includes enhanced benefits over Copilot Free but does not provide the full feature set of Copilot Pro." So the student offer is a separate plan of its own. It is no longer a free copy of Pro.

### Fewer premium models to pick from

The change that annoyed students most was the model list. A GitHub community answer put it this way: "As part of keeping Copilot free for students globally, premium models including Claude Opus, Claude Sonnet, and GPT-5.4 are no longer available for manual selection on student accounts." [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/03/13/microsoft_github_removes_models_student_plan/) covered the removal and the backlash that followed. Other reports say some models are now available only through **Auto** mode, where Copilot picks the model for you.

If you open the model picker and those names are missing, nothing is broken. That's how the plan works now. Third-party summaries of the exact model list disagree, so check the official plans page for the current lineup.

### June: 200 AI Credits a month

GitHub's [student announcement post](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/189268) was updated on 1 June 2026: "Copilot Student plan will now receive 200 monthly included usage AI Credits. Users on the Copilot Student plan will continue to have unlimited code completions."

In practice, that splits Copilot into two parts:

- **Code completions** are the grey inline suggestions that appear as you type. These are unlimited on the Student plan.
- **AI Credits** are a monthly allowance for chat and agent-style work. You get 200 a month.

## Free vs Student vs Pro at a glance

| | Copilot Free | Copilot Student | Copilot Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who gets it | Anyone with a GitHub account | Verified students via GitHub Education | Paid subscribers |
| Cost | Free | Free while you're verified | Paid (see plans page) |
| Code completions | 2,000 per month | Unlimited | See plans page |
| Chat and agent use | Limited allowance of AI Credits | 200 AI Credits per month | Full feature set |
| Premium model choice | Limited | Some premium models not manually selectable; some only via Auto | Fuller access |

Plan details change often. Treat the [official plans page](https://github.com/features/copilot/plans) as the final word.

## Who is eligible

To get Copilot Student through GitHub Education, you need to:

- be **enrolled in a degree- or diploma-granting programme**. University, college and many secondary schools qualify.
- be **13 or older**
- **prove your enrolment**, usually with a school-issued email address. If you don't have one, you can upload documents that show you're currently enrolled.

One detail people often miss: **GitHub reevaluates your eligibility every month.** If your status lapses, for example when you graduate or your enrolment proof expires, your access can change. Keep your academic email and school details up to date in your GitHub settings.

## How to claim Copilot Student, step by step

These steps follow [GitHub Docs](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/copilot-on-github/set-up-copilot/enable-copilot/set-up-for-students).

### Step 1: Get verified (if you aren't already)

1. Sign in to GitHub and go to **github.com/settings/education/benefits**.
2. Click **Start an application**.
3. Choose that you're a student and select your school.
4. Verify with your academic email, or upload proof of enrolment if you don't have one. Use a clear, current document showing your name, your school and a date.
5. Submit and wait for the decision.

A few habits help applications go through:

- Add your school email to your GitHub account before you apply.
- Make sure your GitHub profile name roughly matches the name on your documents.
- Apply from your usual location and network rather than a VPN.

### Step 2: Activate Copilot Student

Being approved does not switch Copilot on by itself. After approval:

1. Go back to **github.com/settings/education/benefits**.
2. Under **"Free GitHub developer resources for students and teachers"**, click **Learn more**.
3. Follow the prompts to activate Copilot Student. Along the way, set your Copilot use policies, such as which features you allow.

### Step 3: Connect your editor

Install the GitHub Copilot extension in your editor (VS Code, a JetBrains IDE, and so on) and sign in with the same GitHub account you verified. Open the model picker to confirm you're on the Student plan.

## Troubleshooting: "I'm verified but still on Copilot Free"

This is the most common complaint in GitHub's community forums, for example [this thread](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/196872). Work through these in order:

1. **Give it time.** Verification and Copilot activation are separate steps, and the student benefit can take several days to apply after you're approved.
2. **Try the direct route.** Open your Copilot settings, or go straight to **github.com/github-copilot/free_signup**.
3. **Check the sign-up status.** If the plans page or docs mention a sign-up pause, that's the cause, not your account.
4. **Contact GitHub Support.** Use the education contact form and pick the **"Claiming Copilot Student benefit"** option. Include your username and the date you were approved.

One more warning: **if you only see paid options, such as a trial or a checkout page asking for card details, don't pay.** The Student plan is free. A payment screen means the benefit hasn't reached your account yet.

## Getting the most out of 200 AI Credits

Completions are unlimited, so you only need to manage your credits. A few habits make them last the month:

- **Use completions for routine code.** Let the inline suggestions handle boilerplate, loops and tests. They don't touch your credits.
- **Save chat for real questions,** like understanding an error, a concept or a design choice.
- **Write tight prompts.** Paste the relevant function and the exact error. Pasting a whole file and asking "why doesn't this work?" wastes credits.
- **Go easy on agent tasks.** Multi-step agent work is where an allowance goes fastest.
- **Keep an eye on usage** in your Copilot settings, especially near deadlines.

A note on coursework: free access doesn't mean every assignment allows AI help. Rules vary by module. Read our **Using AI for Coursework** guide for academic integrity and citation rules before you use Copilot on graded work.

## What else is in the Student Developer Pack

Copilot Student is one part of the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack). Once you're verified, you also get:

- **GitHub Pro** free while you remain verified
- **Codespaces** hours for cloud development environments
- **Partner offers** such as hosting, domains and dev tools. Each one is claimed separately from the pack page.

Partner offers change often, so check the terms of each one on the pack page before relying on it.

## Quick FAQ

### Is GitHub Copilot still free for students in 2026?
Yes. Verified students get the Copilot Student plan free through GitHub Education. It includes unlimited code completions and 200 AI Credits a month, but not the full Copilot Pro feature set.

### Why can't I select Claude Opus, Claude Sonnet or GPT-5.4?
Since March 2026, those premium models aren't available for manual selection on student accounts, and some models are only reachable through Auto mode. This is expected behaviour.

### Can I apply if I'm in high school?
Yes, if you're 13 or older, enrolled in a degree- or diploma-granting programme, and can prove your enrolment.

### How long does activation take?
Approval and activation are separate steps. The benefit can take several days to appear after verification. If it still hasn't appeared after that, use the troubleshooting steps above or contact GitHub Support.

### What happens when I graduate?
GitHub rechecks eligibility every month. Once you're no longer an enrolled student, expect your student benefits to end, and check the plans page for your options.
