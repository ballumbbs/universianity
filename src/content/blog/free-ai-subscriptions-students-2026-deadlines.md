Two of the biggest student AI offers of the year close soon. Google's free year of AI Pro (US) or AI Plus (most other countries) has a claim deadline of **31 December 2026**. OpenAI's free ChatGPT Plus offer for US students reportedly closes on **31 October 2026**, which is only days away.

Both offers have something in common: **they turn into paid subscriptions automatically** unless you cancel. Free is great, but only if you don't forget about it.

This guide covers who qualifies, how to claim, and how to avoid a surprise charge next year.

> **A note on sources:** The details below come from news reports and independent guides published since Google's August launch. Google and OpenAI can change terms at any time, and some reports disagree on specifics. Before you enter any card details, confirm eligibility, deadlines and prices on Google's official Gemini for students page and OpenAI's official offer page.

## Urgent: ChatGPT Plus for US students (reported deadline 31 October)

If you study in the US, deal with this one first.

- **What:** Four free monthly billing periods of ChatGPT Plus as part of OpenAI's 2026 Back to School offer, according to [Krater's breakdown of the terms](https://krater.ai/blog/chatgpt-plus-student-discount).
- **Who:** Full-time and part-time students at eligible US degree-granting institutions.
- **How:** Student verification through SheerID, plus a payment method on file.
- **After the free period:** It renews at **$20/month**.
- **Outside the US:** This offer isn't available. Your options are paying full price or using **ChatGPT Edu** if your university provides it. Ask your IT or library team.

If you claim it, set a calendar reminder for a few days before the fourth billing period ends. Check OpenAI's own page to confirm the exact cut-off before you rely on it.

## The main offer: a free year of Google AI

Google announced its 2026 student offer on 19–20 August. [MacRumors reported on launch day](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/19/students-free-year-google-ai-pro/) that the offer splits by country:

- **US college students:** 12 months of **Google AI Pro** free, normally $19.99/month.
- **Eligible students outside the US:** 12 months of **Google AI Plus** free.

You must **redeem by 31 December 2026**. The 12 months start when you claim, not on 31 December.

### What you get

Reported Google AI Pro features include:

- Higher usage limits in the Gemini app
- Gemini inside Google apps such as Gmail and Google Docs
- Extra cloud storage

Reports disagree on how much storage each plan includes. Some figures are from last year's offer, which had different terms. Check Google's page for the current amount instead of trusting a number from a blog.

AI Plus is the lighter, cheaper tier. According to [ExplainX](https://explainx.ai/blog/google-gemini-free-student-plan-ai-pro-plus-august-2026), it covers 140+ countries, and the launch came with a new student hub that includes study notebooks and flashcards.

### Which countries get AI Plus?

Coverage is broad, but it isn't everywhere. Examples from the reporting:

- [27 Sub-Saharan African countries, including Ghana](https://opportunitiesforyouth.org/2026/08/21/google-ai-plus-student-plan-2026-free-1-year-ai-access-for-tertiary-students-in-ghana-and-26-other-african-countries/)
- [A Saudi Arabia rollout aimed at 1 million students](https://shattered.io/saudi-arabia-google-ai-plus-1-million-students-2026/)

Some guides say a small number of countries are excluded, but the published lists don't match. **Check Google's page for your own country** before you assume you're in or out.

One important rule: according to [this GlobalGPT guide](https://www.glbgpt.com/hub/gemini-3-student-guide/), **the plan you get follows your institution's country or region**, not where you are right now. A US-enrolled student abroad is treated as US. An international student at a UK university is treated as UK.

## Who qualifies for the Google offer

Independent guides list these requirements:

1. **You're a current student at an eligible institution**, verified through SheerID.
2. **You're 18 or over.** [PerkStack](https://perkstack.co/blog/google-ai-pro-free-students) gives this as the age rule for US college students. Confirm it on the official page.
3. **You use a personal Google Account.** The offer reportedly doesn't work on school-issued Google Workspace accounts.
4. **You add a qualifying payment method.** You won't be charged during the free year, but a card is needed for the renewal.

### Already claimed last year?

Good news, according to [Credit for Startups](https://creditforstartups.com/students/gemini-student): if your free AI Pro trial from 2025 has **expired**, you can claim again. Google re-checks eligibility every year, so you'll need to verify as a student again.

## How to claim Google AI Pro or Plus: step by step

1. **Pick the right account.** Use a personal Gmail you'll still have after graduation, not your university address. Your files and settings stay with that account.
2. **Check for existing trials.** Per [Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/gemini-student-offer), claiming the student offer **ends any other Google One extended trial** on that account. If you're partway through one, decide which you want.
3. **Open Google's official Gemini for students page** while signed into that personal account.
4. **Verify through SheerID.** Enter your institution's name. You may be asked for a student email confirmation or a document such as an enrolment letter or student ID. Use your institution's official name exactly as it appears.
5. **Add your payment method** when prompted.
6. **Check which plan you got.** It should show AI Pro if you're US-based and AI Plus elsewhere, along with the date your free period ends.
7. **Set your cancellation reminder now** (see below).

If verification fails, the usual causes are a mismatch between the institution name and your documents, or trying to claim on a Workspace account. Fix those before trying again.

## Don't get billed by accident

After the free period, reported renewal prices are:

| Plan | Free period | Renews at |
|---|---|---|
| Google AI Pro (US) | 12 months | $19.99/month |
| Google AI Plus (outside US) | 12 months | $4.99/month or local equivalent |
| ChatGPT Plus (US) | 4 billing periods | $20/month |

Two simple habits:

- **Set a reminder a few days before the end date.** For Google, that's about 11 months and 3 weeks after you claim. For ChatGPT, it's just before your fourth billing period ends.
- **When the reminder goes off, decide on purpose.** If the tool has earned its place in your week, keep it. If not, cancel. These are temporary promotions, not permanent free tiers.

## Use it well, and within the rules

A free year of AI doesn't change your university's academic integrity policy. Before you use any of these tools on assessed work, **read your institution's and your module's AI policy**. Rules can differ between courses, and even between assignments.

The safest high-value uses are the ones that help you learn:

- Turning your own lecture notes into flashcards
- Quizzing yourself before exams
- Getting explanations of concepts you're stuck on

## Quick FAQ

**Can I claim Google's offer with my university email?**
Reportedly not if it's a Google Workspace account. Use a personal Google Account you'll keep after graduation.

**I'm an international student in the US. Do I get Pro or Plus?**
The plan reportedly follows your institution's country, so a US institution should mean AI Pro. Confirm on Google's page.

**Do I have to use the free year starting 31 December?**
No. The 12 months start when you claim. 31 December is the last day you can claim.

**Can I get ChatGPT Plus free if I study outside the US?**
Not through this offer. Check whether your university provides ChatGPT Edu.

**Will I be charged if I forget to cancel?**
Yes. Both offers renew as paid plans, so set a reminder for a few days before the free period ends.

If you study in the US, check OpenAI's official offer page today and decide whether to claim ChatGPT Plus before 31 October.
